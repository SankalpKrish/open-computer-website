## Platform

## Users
A balanced mix of engineering/product teams and business/operations leaders, confirmed by the user.

## Product Purpose
Open Computer is an AI-native product studio and engineering lab. This site presents Athena Voice, Open Computer Agent, Inferno Router, and their implementation services.

## Capabilities and Constraints
All public product facts, partner names, descriptions, and contact details come from `lib/site-content.ts` and existing page modules. Preserve dedicated product routes, services, contact, privacy, and terms. The contact form opens an email draft; it does not send or store messages.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the Open Computer name and existing product facts. Maintain three developed website styles, Studio, Editorial, and Greek, with a persistent switcher. Greek lives in `themes/greek` and is built from the business card: the Georgia Pro wordmark and the Athena engraving. Botanical, Terminal, and Blueprint are retired. References include 360 Labs, Vercel, Framer, and Supabase: aim for comparable product clarity and polish with an independent visual identity. The former OpenSlides-like appearance is an anti-reference. Keep copy simple like 360 Labs: direct headings, concise product descriptions, and useful controls. Avoid repeated taglines, decorative section labels, preview captions, and CTA footnotes.

## Evidence on Hand
The codebase supplies product details, partners and clients, a 90-day pilot, FAQs, and local visual assets. Do not invent testimonials, metrics, logos, or commercial claims.

## Component Sources
Preserve the overall theme and page flow of Studio and Editorial. Source new UI components from [RareUI](https://www.rareui.com/components) first; use [Designeer](https://designeer.xyz/) only if RareUI has no suitable component. Keep upstream notices, the RareUI license, and a visible attribution link in the README, as permitted by the license. Product illustrations must not imply a live backend session.
