## Direction contract

THESIS: The stack is a temple. Three products stand as three classical orders under one entablature, on steps inscribed "on your infrastructure". It refuses the split hero (copy beside a product shot) and the centred headline over three equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Marble white ground (#FBFBF9), Pentelic secondary surface, hairline veins, the card's engraving grey (#737373) for drawings, wordmark and bust, near-black ink, and one pigment: Egyptian blue (#1034A6), drenched once as the painted frieze ground and used for primary actions. Georgia Pro (Georgia, then Gelasio) for every reading voice; Jost capitals for inscriptions. 0px radius, no shadows, hairline architectural rules, triglyphs, fluted rules, interpuncts.

STORY: The visitor sees the company as a building: Athena in the pediment, the headline on the architrave, Voice/Agent/Router as Ionic/Doric/Corinthian columns on their infrastructure. They scroll into a blue frieze of product metopes, the partners inscribed on an architrave band, a stepped method (Listen, Act, Verify), questions, and a close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The full facade, centred, at most 1040px wide. A shallow pediment holding the bust; cornice and triglyph frieze; architrave with the H1 "Products you can run." at up to 76px; three drawn columns (each a link, labelled on the stylobate); thin steps; the subline and two actions (primary Egyptian blue "Explore the products", outline "Build with us") directly beneath. Everything fits within 1280×720.

FORM: Engraved architectural elevation (the plate tradition of Stuart & Revett), ranked first of the user-pinned world. Signature interaction: polychromy.
